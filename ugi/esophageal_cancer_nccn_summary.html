Radiation Therapy Volumes & Constraints
Target volume definitions, dose limits, and dose recommendations from NCCN v2.2026.
Target volume definitions
GTV (Gross Tumor Volume)
Primary tumor + involved regional lymph nodes as identified by pre-treatment diagnostic studies.
CTV (Clinical Target Volume)
Primary tumor + 3–4 cm superior & inferior expansion + 1 cm radial expansion.

Nodal CTV: 0.5–1.5 cm expansion from nodal GTV. Include elective nodal regions (e.g., celiac axis) based on primary tumor location.
PTV (Planning Target Volume)
CTV + 0.5–1 cm expansion margin to account for setup uncertainty and organ motion.
Dose recommendations
Preoperative chemoRT
41.4 Gy (CROSS regimen) with concurrent paclitaxel + carboplatin. Standard of care for resectable locoregional disease.
Cat 1 preferredBefore surgery
Definitive chemoRT
50–50.4 Gy. ARTDECO and CONCORDE trials showed no benefit to dose escalation beyond 50 Gy. INT-0123 showed no benefit at 64.8 Gy vs. 50.4 Gy.
50–50.4 Gy standardNo escalation benefit
Normal tissue dose limits
Heart Minimize RT dose to the left ventricle. Use DVH parameters as guidance.
Lungs Use lung DVH parameters as predictors of pulmonary complications. Consensus on optimal criteria not yet established — see algorithm for NCCN recommended criteria.
Liver / kidneys / cord Minimize unnecessary RT doses. Volume of organs at risk receiving high RT doses should be limited.
Note Dose guidelines may be exceeded based on clinical circumstances when required for tumor coverage.
Preoperative & Perioperative Trials
Landmark trials establishing preoperative chemoRT and perioperative chemotherapy strategies.
Phase III preoperative chemoRT trials
CROSS trial
Paclitaxel + carboplatin + 41.4 Gy RT → surgery vs. surgery alone. n=366; 75% adenocarcinoma, 23% SCC. Resectable T2–T3, N0–1, M0.
OS: 49 vs 24 mo HR 0.657 (P=.003) R0: 92% vs 69% pCR: 29% overall
Established paclitaxel + carboplatin as CATEGORY 1 PREFERRED preop chemoRT regimen. Long-term follow-up (84-month median) confirmed median OS 48.6 vs 24 months (HR 0.68, P=.003).
CALGB 9781
Fluorouracil + cisplatin + RT → surgery vs. surgery alone. n=56, stage I–III esophageal cancer. Median follow-up 6 years.
Median OS: 4.5 vs 1.8 yr P=.002 5-yr OS: 39% vs 16%
Reflects long-term survival advantage for preop chemoRT with fluorouracil + cisplatin. Fluorouracil + cisplatin remains a category 1 recommended option.
NEOCRTEC5010
Preop chemoRT + surgery vs. surgery alone in locally advanced esophageal SCC. n=451 (224 preop chemoRT, 227 surgery alone).
OS: 100.1 vs 66.5 mo HR 0.71 (P=.025) R0: 98.4% vs 91.2% DFS: 100.1 vs 41.7 mo
Confirms OS and DFS benefit of preop chemoRT over surgery alone in locally advanced SCC with acceptable toxicity. Postoperative complications similar between groups.
NEO-AEGIS trial
CROSS (preop chemoRT) vs. modified MAGIC or FLOT (periop chemo) in locoregional esophageal or EGJ adenocarcinoma. n=377; median follow-up 24.5 months.
3-yr OS: 56% vs 57% HR 1.02 (no difference)
All pathologic endpoints (pCR, N0, R0) favored preop chemoRT. Strongly suggests NONINFERIORITY of perioperative chemotherapy to preop chemoRT for adenocarcinoma — both valid options.
Stahl et al. (SCC)
Induction chemo → preop chemoRT + surgery vs. induction chemo → chemoRT alone in esophageal SCC. n=172.
2-yr PFS: 64.3% vs 40.7% OS: no difference Treatment mortality: 12.8% vs 3.5%
Surgery improves locoregional control but not OS in SCC. Addition of surgery associated with higher treatment-related mortality in SCC patients who respond to chemoradiation.
Checkmate-577
Nivolumab vs. placebo post-preop chemoRT + R0 resection with residual pathologic disease. N=794 (532 nivo, 262 placebo).
Median DFS: 22.4 vs 11 mo HR 0.69 (P<.001)
DFS benefit regardless of PD-L1 expression levels. Grade 3–4 AEs: 13% nivo vs 6% placebo. ESTABLISHED adjuvant nivolumab as CATEGORY 1 PREFERRED postoperative therapy.
Perioperative chemotherapy trials
MAGIC trial
Periop ECF (epirubicin + cisplatin + fluorouracil) vs. surgery alone in non-metastatic stage II+ gastric or EGJ adenocarcinoma.
Improved PFS & OSECF no longer recommended
First landmark trial demonstrating perioperative chemotherapy benefit. ECF was the standard but has been replaced by FLOT/FOLFOX.
FLOT4 (phase III)
FLOT (fluorouracil + leucovorin + oxaliplatin + docetaxel) vs. ECF perioperative in resectable gastric or EGJ adenocarcinoma ≥cT2 or N+. n=716 phase III.
OS: 50 vs 35 mo HR 0.77 pCR: 16% vs 6%
FLOT superior to ECF; ECF no longer recommended. However, considerable toxicity with FLOT — panel recommends for select patients with GOOD PS. FOLFOX preferred for most patients with good-to-moderate PS.
FNCLCC ACCORD 07
Periop fluorouracil + cisplatin vs. surgery alone. n=224; 75% adenocarcinoma of lower esophagus or EGJ. Median follow-up 5.7 years.
5-yr OS: 38% vs 24%P=.02
Confirmed survival benefit of perioperative fluorouracil + cisplatin. Panel considers it a viable treatment option for locally advanced resectable esophageal or EGJ cancers.
Definitive Chemoradiation Trials
Evidence base for definitive chemoRT in unresectable or medically inoperable esophageal cancer.
Landmark definitive chemoRT trials
RTOG 85-01
Chemoradiation (fluorouracil + cisplatin + RT) vs. RT alone in esophageal SCC or adenocarcinoma (cT1–cT3, N0–1, M0).
Median OS: 14 vs 9 mo 5-yr OS: 27% vs 0% 8-yr OS: 22%
Established concurrent chemoradiation as the standard approach over RT alone for esophageal cancer. Local failure also significantly reduced in chemoRT arm (47% vs 65%).
INT-0123 (RTOG 94-05)
Standard RT 50.4 Gy vs. high-dose 64.8 Gy with same fluorouracil + cisplatin chemotherapy. n=218.
OS: 13 vs 18 mo (P=NS) No benefit to dose escalation
No significant difference in median survival, 2-year OS, or locoregional failure between high-dose and standard-dose RT. Supports use of 50–50.4 Gy as standard.
PRODIGE5/ACCORD17
FOLFOX vs. fluorouracil + cisplatin in definitive chemoRT for unresectable or medically unfit esophageal cancer. n=267.
PFS: 9.7 vs 9.4 mo P=.64 (no difference)
FOLFOX preferred over fluorouracil + cisplatin for definitive chemoRT due to FEWER TREATMENT-RELATED ADVERSE EVENTS. Both are category 1 recommendations.
ARTDECO trial
Dose escalation (61.6 Gy to GTV) vs. standard 50.4 Gy with concurrent paclitaxel + carboplatin. Recently completed phase III.
No benefit to dose escalation
Confirmed no local control or survival benefit from RT dose escalation beyond 50 Gy. Supports standard 50–50.4 Gy for definitive chemoRT.
CONCORDE (PRODIGE-26)
Phase III trial comparing escalated RT dose vs. standard 50 Gy in definitive chemoRT for esophageal cancer.
No benefit to dose escalation
Similarly showed no improvement in local control or survival with RT dose escalation beyond 50 Gy. Consistent with ARTDECO results.
INT-0116 (postoperative)
Surgery + postop chemoRT (bolus fluorouracil + leucovorin + RT) vs. surgery alone in resected gastric or EGJ adenocarcinoma. n=556, stage IB–IV M0.
Median OS: 36 vs 27 mo P=.005 Grade 3–4 hemato AEs: 54%
Established postop chemoRT efficacy. However, the INT-0116 dosing schedule causes HIGH TOXICITY (17% discontinued; 3 deaths from toxicities). Doses/schedule used in this trial are NOT recommended by panel — see algorithm for modifications.
Metastatic & Advanced Disease Trials
First-line, second-line, and subsequent therapy evidence for advanced esophageal and EGJ cancer.
First-line systemic therapy
Checkmate-649
Nivolumab + chemo (capecitabine + oxaliplatin or mFOLFOX6) vs. chemo alone in HER2-negative, previously untreated gastric, EGJ, or esophageal adenocarcinoma. n=1581.
OS (CPS≥5): 14.4 vs 11.1 mo HR 0.71 (P<.0001) OS (all pts): 13.8 vs 11.6 mo
Nivolumab + fluoropyrimidine + oxaliplatin is CATEGORY 1 preferred for HER2-negative adenocarcinoma with PD-L1 CPS≥5. Category 2B for CPS<5.
CheckMate-648
Nivolumab + chemo OR nivolumab + ipilimumab vs. chemo alone in previously untreated unresectable/recurrent/metastatic esophageal SCC. n=970.
OS (PD-L1≥1%): 15 vs 9 mo (nivo+chemo) HR 0.54 (P<.001) OS (PD-L1≥1%): 14 vs 9 mo (nivo+ipi)
Both nivo+chemo AND nivo+ipilimumab are preferred for SCC. 91% of patients had tumor cell PD-L1≥1%. NCCN recommends both regimens IRRESPECTIVE OF CPS SCORE for SCC.
KEYNOTE-590
Pembrolizumab + chemo vs. placebo + chemo in locally advanced/metastatic esophageal SCC, esophageal adeno, or EGJ adeno. n=749.
OS (SCC + CPS≥10): 13.9 vs 8.8 mo HR 0.57 (P<.0001) OS (all pts): 12.4 vs 9.8 mo
Pembrolizumab + chemo approved for SCC or adenocarcinoma with PD-L1 CPS≥10 (category 1 with cisplatin) or CPS<10 (category 2B).
ToGA trial
Trastuzumab + chemo (cisplatin + fluorouracil or capecitabine) vs. chemo alone in HER2-overexpression positive advanced gastric or EGJ adenocarcinoma. n=594.
Median OS: 13.8 vs 11 mo P=.046 Best in IHC 3+ or IHC 2+/FISH+
Established trastuzumab + fluoropyrimidine + platinum as STANDARD for HER2+ advanced gastroesophageal adenocarcinoma. Do NOT continue trastuzumab in 2nd-line therapy.
KEYNOTE-811
Pembrolizumab vs. placebo added to trastuzumab + fluorouracil/capecitabine + cisplatin/oxaliplatin in HER2+ gastric or EGJ adenocarcinoma. Interim analysis, n=264.
ORR: 74% vs 52% P=.00006 CR: 11% vs 3%
Pembrolizumab combined with trastuzumab + fluoropyrimidine + platinum is a PREFERRED OPTION for HER2+ advanced adenocarcinoma. Similar AE incidence in both arms (57%).
FLO / FOLFOX vs. FLP
FOLFOX vs. fluorouracil + cisplatin (FLP) in previously untreated advanced gastric or EGJ adenocarcinoma. n=220.
ORR: 41.3% vs 16.7% Improved OS in pts >65 yr Less toxicity vs. cisplatin
FOLFOX offers reduced toxicity and similar/improved efficacy vs. fluorouracil + cisplatin, especially in older patients. Oxaliplatin preferred over cisplatin due to lower toxicity.
Second-line and subsequent therapy
ATTRACTION-3
Nivolumab vs. chemotherapy (docetaxel or paclitaxel) in advanced esophageal SCC refractory to fluoropyrimidine- and platinum-based therapy. n=419.
Median OS: 10.9 vs 8.4 mo P=.019 Grade 3–4 AEs: 18% vs 63%
OS benefit regardless of PD-L1 expression. CATEGORY 1 recommendation; nivolumab is preferred 2nd-line for advanced esophageal SCC. Favorable safety profile.
KEYNOTE-181
Pembrolizumab vs. investigator's choice chemotherapy as 2nd-line in advanced SCC or adenocarcinoma of esophagus/EGJ. n=628.
OS (SCC + CPS≥10): 9.3 vs 6.7 mo P=.007 Grade 3–5 AEs: 18% vs 41%
Pembrolizumab is CATEGORY 1 PREFERRED 2nd-line therapy for advanced esophageal SCC with PD-L1 CPS≥10. Durable clinical benefit in high PD-L1 expressors.
REGARD trial
Ramucirumab vs. placebo in advanced gastric or EGJ adenocarcinoma after 1st-line platinum or fluoropyrimidine-based chemotherapy. n=355.
Median OS: 5.2 vs 3.8 mo P=.047
First survival benefit demonstrated for ramucirumab as single agent. Category 1 for EGJ adenocarcinoma; category 2A for esophageal adenocarcinoma.
RAINBOW trial
Ramucirumab + paclitaxel vs. paclitaxel alone in metastatic gastric or EGJ adenocarcinoma progressing after 1st-line. n=665.
Median OS: 9.6 vs 7.4 mo P<.0001 ORR: 28% vs 16%
Ramucirumab + paclitaxel (preferred) approved for 2nd-line treatment of advanced gastric or EGJ adenocarcinoma. Ramucirumab is a significant predictor of OS and PFS.
DESTINY-Gastric01
Fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki vs. physician's choice chemotherapy in HER2+ gastric or EGJ adenocarcinoma with ≥2 prior lines including trastuzumab. n=188.
Median OS: 12.5 vs 8.4 mo ORR: 40.5% vs 11% P=.0097
Fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki approved for 2nd-line+ HER2+ adenocarcinoma. Monitor for ILD/pneumonitis (12 patients; 1 drug-related death). Careful patient selection required.
TAGS trial
Trifluridine + tipiracil vs. placebo in heavily pretreated metastatic gastric or EGJ adenocarcinoma. n=507 (randomized 2:1).
OS improvement: +2.1 mo HR 0.69 (P=.0003) Grade 3–4 neutropenia: 38%
Category 1 preferred for 3rd-line recurrent/metastatic EGJ adeno. No partial/complete responses; consider only for low-volume disease patients with minimal symptoms who can swallow pills.
Targeted Therapies
FDA-approved targeted agents for advanced esophageal and EGJ cancer, with mechanisms and indications.
Checkpoint inhibitors
Nivolumab Anti-PD-1
3 approved settings: adjuvant (post-preop chemoRT), 1st-line metastatic, 2nd-line SCC
Adjuvant: cat 1 preferred 1st-line adeno: CPS≥5 cat 1 2nd-line SCC: cat 1
Adjuvant use post-preop chemoRT + R0 resection for residual disease. 1st-line: combined with fluoropyrimidine + oxaliplatin or ipilimumab for SCC. 2nd-line: proven benefit in SCC regardless of PD-L1 (ATTRACTION-3). Approved May & April 2021, June 2020, May 2022.
Pembrolizumab Anti-PD-1
1st-line and 2nd-line; multiple biomarker-driven indications
CPS≥10: cat 1 (cisplatin) MSI-H/dMMR: any line TMB-H: 2nd-line+
1st-line with chemo for SCC or adeno with CPS≥10 (cat 1) or CPS<10 (cat 2B). Add to trastuzumab + chemo for HER2+ adeno (KEYNOTE-811). 2nd-line preferred for SCC CPS≥10. Tissue-agnostic: MSI-H/dMMR (2017) and TMB-H (2020) approvals.
HER2-targeted
Trastuzumab HER2 / ErbB2
1st-line for HER2-overexpression positive advanced adenocarcinoma
IHC 3+ or IHC 2+/FISH+ Cat 1 preferred (1st-line)
Combine with fluoropyrimidine + platinum (oxaliplatin preferred over cisplatin due to lower toxicity). FDA-approved biosimilar is appropriate substitute. DO NOT continue in 2nd-line therapy. May combine with pembrolizumab based on KEYNOTE-811.
Fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki ADC · HER2
2nd-line+ for HER2+ adenocarcinoma after prior trastuzumab (DESTINY-Gastric01)
2nd-line+ HER2+ Post-trastuzumab failure
Antibody-drug conjugate: trastuzumab + cytotoxic topoisomerase I inhibitor. ORR 40.5% vs 11% chemotherapy. OS 12.5 vs 8.4 months. Monitor for ILD/pneumonitis. Careful patient selection and close toxicity monitoring required.
Anti-angiogenic
Ramucirumab VEGFR-2
2nd-line for advanced gastric or EGJ adenocarcinoma (REGARD, RAINBOW trials)
Cat 1 (EGJ adeno) Cat 2A (esophageal adeno)
Single agent or in combination with paclitaxel (preferred). RAINFALL trial showed NO benefit for ramucirumab in 1st-line treatment-naïve patients — NOT recommended 1st-line. Higher hypertension rates vs. placebo (16% vs 8%).
Tumor-agnostic / biomarker-driven
Entrectinib / Larotrectinib TRK inhibitor · NTRK
NTRK gene fusion-positive solid tumors (NTRK1, NTRK2, or NTRK3). Extremely rare in gastroesophageal cancers.
NTRK1/2/3 gene fusion Larotrectinib: ORR 75% Entrectinib: ORR 57%
Larotrectinib approved 2018 for patients ≥12 years with NTRK fusion-positive solid tumors without known resistance mutation. Entrectinib approved 2019 for NTRK fusion-positive tumors and ROS1+ NSCLC. Response duration ≥6 months in ~70%+.
Dabrafenib + Trametinib B-Raf + MEK
BRAF V600E mutated unresectable or metastatic solid tumors (tumor agnostic, FDA June 2022)
BRAF V600E mutation ORR 38–41% Median OS 29 mo (NCI-MATCH)
Based on BRF117019 and NCI-MATCH (EAY131-H) trials. PFS 11.4 months (NCI-MATCH). Common AEs: pyrexia, fatigue, nausea, rash, chills, headache, hemorrhage, cough, vomiting.
Selpercatinib RET kinase inhibitor
RET gene fusion-positive locally advanced or metastatic solid tumors (tumor agnostic, FDA September 2022)
RET gene fusion ORR 44% (LIBRETTO-001)
Based on interim analysis of LIBRETTO-001 trial (41 patients with RET fusion-positive tumors, non-NSCLC/non-thyroid). Tyrosine kinase inhibitor.
Dostarlimab-gxly Anti-PD-1
MSI-H or dMMR recurrent or advanced solid tumors progressing after prior therapy (FDA August 2021)
dMMR / MSI-H No prior PD-1/PD-L1 ORR 42% (GARNET)
GARNET trial: 209 patients with dMMR solid tumors (majority endometrial or GI cancers). Median response duration 35 months. 9% complete response rate, 33% partial response. Common AEs: fatigue, anemia, diarrhea, nausea; immune-mediated AEs possible.

Clinical recommendations
Preop chemoRT is preferred for resectable locoregional disease
Paclitaxel + carboplatin (CROSS regimen, 41.4 Gy) and FOLFOX + RT are both CATEGORY 1 PREFERRED. Perioperative chemotherapy (FOLFOX preferred for most; FLOT for select good-PS patients) is a valid, noninferior alternative for adenocarcinoma per NEO-AEGIS. Irinotecan + cisplatin (cat 2B) and paclitaxel + fluoropyrimidine (cat 2B) are other preop options.
Adjuvant nivolumab is category 1 preferred post-resection with residual disease
For patients with completely resected esophageal or EGJ cancer with residual pathologic disease following preop chemoRT, nivolumab (Checkmate-577) significantly improves DFS (22.4 vs. 11 months; HR 0.69). DFS benefit observed regardless of PD-L1 expression levels. This is the new standard of care.
Definitive chemoRT uses FOLFOX preferred at 50–50.4 Gy
Reserved for unresectable disease or patients declining surgery. FOLFOX preferred over fluorouracil + cisplatin (fewer adverse events, comparable efficacy; PRODIGE5/ACCORD17). Radiation dose escalation beyond 50 Gy has NOT shown survival benefit in 3 trials (INT-0123, ARTDECO, CONCORDE). Retrospective data support paclitaxel + carboplatin as another option.
Biomarker testing is mandatory before initiating metastatic systemic therapy
Test HER2 (IHC/FISH), PD-L1 by CPS, MSI by PCR/NGS, MMR by IHC, TMB by NGS, NTRK fusion, RET fusion, and BRAF V600E. Use IHC/ISH/PCR first, then NGS as appropriate. When tissue is limited, comprehensive genomic profiling via a validated NGS assay in a CLIA-approved laboratory may be used.
1st-line metastatic: immunotherapy + chemotherapy is the new backbone
HER2-negative adenocarcinoma: nivolumab + fluoropyrimidine + oxaliplatin (CPS≥5 cat 1; CPS<5 cat 2B). Esophageal SCC: nivolumab + chemo OR nivolumab + ipilimumab — BOTH preferred REGARDLESS of PD-L1/CPS score. Pembrolizumab + chemo for SCC or adeno with CPS≥10 (cat 1 with cisplatin). Oxaliplatin preferred over cisplatin in most regimens due to lower toxicity.
HER2-positive adenocarcinoma: trastuzumab + chemo ± pembrolizumab is standard
Add trastuzumab (or FDA-approved biosimilar) to fluoropyrimidine + platinum (oxaliplatin preferred). Pembrolizumab can also be added based on KEYNOTE-811 interim data (ORR 74% vs. 52%). Do NOT continue trastuzumab beyond 1st-line. In 2nd-line after trastuzumab failure, consider fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki (DESTINY-Gastric01).
2nd-line depends on histology, prior therapy, and biomarkers
SCC: nivolumab (cat 1, regardless of PD-L1; ATTRACTION-3) or pembrolizumab (CPS≥10 cat 1; KEYNOTE-181). Adenocarcinoma: ramucirumab ± paclitaxel (REGARD/RAINBOW). HER2+: fam-trastuzumab deruxtecan-nxki (DESTINY-Gastric01). MSI-H/dMMR: pembrolizumab or dostarlimab-gxly. 3 cytotoxic drugs only for medically fit patients with excellent PS.
RT planning: strict volume definitions and cardiac dose minimization
GTV: primary tumor + involved nodes. CTV: +3–4 cm sup/inf, +1 cm radial (primary); +0.5–1.5 cm from nodal GTV (nodal). Include elective nodal regions (celiac axis) based on primary location. PTV: CTV + 0.5–1 cm. Minimize left ventricle dose. Use lung DVH parameters to predict pulmonary complications. Dose guidelines may be exceeded based on clinical circumstances.
